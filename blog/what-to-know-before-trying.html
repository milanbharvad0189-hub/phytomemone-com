What Phyto Thrive Labs Actually Contains

Phyto Thrive Labs lists 10 named ingredients on its label. Here is what each one is included for:

  • Vitamin B12 — Vitamin B12 plays a key role in maintaining healthy nerve cells and is involved in the formation of red blood cells. It is often included in cognitive supplements because low levels have been linked to fatigue and cognitive dullness.
  • Vitamin B6 — Vitamin B6 is necessary for the synthesis of neurotransmitters like serotonin and dopamine, which influence mood and mental sharpness. It also supports the nervous system and helps convert food into energy.
  • Ginkgo Biloba — Ginkgo biloba is a well-known botanical that has been studied for its potential to support blood flow to the brain and antioxidant activity. It is commonly found in memory and focus formulas.
  • Lion’s Mane Mushroom — Lion’s Mane is a medicinal mushroom that has gained popularity for its possible neuroprotective properties. It is believed to support nerve growth factor and overall brain health.
  • Bacopa Monnieri — Bacopa monnieri is an adaptogenic herb used in traditional Ayurvedic medicine to support memory, concentration, and learning. It is often included in nootropic supplements for its cognitive-enhancing potential.
  • Phosphatidylserine — Phosphatidylserine is a phospholipid found in cell membranes, particularly in the brain. It is thought to support communication between neurons and may help maintain cognitive function with age.
  • Alpha-GPC — Alpha-GPC is a natural choline compound that can cross the blood-brain barrier and may support the production of acetylcholine, a neurotransmitter involved in memory and learning.
  • Huperzine A — Huperzine A is a compound extracted from the Chinese club moss that has been studied for its ability to inhibit acetylcholinesterase, potentially increasing acetylcholine levels in the brain for better memory and focus.
