What Are The Ingredients In Phyto Thrive Labs?

Phyto Thrive Labs is formulated with 10 named ingredients — including Vitamin B12, Vitamin B6, Ginkgo Biloba, Lion’s Mane Mushroom, Bacopa Monnieri — each listed individually below with the role it plays in the formula, so you can see exactly what you are taking before you buy.

  • 1

    Vitamin B12

    ESSENTIAL NUTRIENT FOR NERVE FUNCTION AND ENERGY METABOLISM

    Vitamin B12 plays a key role in maintaining healthy nerve cells and is involved in the formation of red blood cells. It is often included in cognitive supplements because low levels have been linked to fatigue and cognitive dullness.

  • 2

    Vitamin B6

    SUPPORTS NEUROTRANSMITTER PRODUCTION AND BRAIN FUNCTION

    Vitamin B6 is necessary for the synthesis of neurotransmitters like serotonin and dopamine, which influence mood and mental sharpness. It also supports the nervous system and helps convert food into energy.

  • 3

    Ginkgo Biloba

    HERBAL EXTRACT TRADITIONALLY USED FOR MEMORY SUPPORT

    Ginkgo biloba is a well-known botanical that has been studied for its potential to support blood flow to the brain and antioxidant activity. It is commonly found in memory and focus formulas.

  • 4

    Lion’s Mane Mushroom

    BOTANICAL FOR COGNITIVE AND NERVE SUPPORT

    Lion’s Mane is a medicinal mushroom that has gained popularity for its possible neuroprotective properties. It is believed to support nerve growth factor and overall brain health.

  • 5

    Bacopa Monnieri

    AYURVEDIC HERB FOR MEMORY AND LEARNING

    Bacopa monnieri is an adaptogenic herb used in traditional Ayurvedic medicine to support memory, concentration, and learning. It is often included in nootropic supplements for its cognitive-enhancing potential.

  • 6

    Phosphatidylserine

    PHOSPHOLIPID FOR CELL MEMBRANE HEALTH

    Phosphatidylserine is a phospholipid found in cell membranes, particularly in the brain. It is thought to support communication between neurons and may help maintain cognitive function with age.

  • 7

    Alpha-GPC

    CHOLINE COMPOUND FOR NEUROTRANSMITTER SUPPORT

    Alpha-GPC is a natural choline compound that can cross the blood-brain barrier and may support the production of acetylcholine, a neurotransmitter involved in memory and learning.

  • 8

    Huperzine A

    ALKALOID THAT MAY SUPPORT ACETYLCHOLINE LEVELS

    Huperzine A is a compound extracted from the Chinese club moss that has been studied for its ability to inhibit acetylcholinesterase, potentially increasing acetylcholine levels in the brain for better memory and focus.

  • 9

    Acetyl-L-Carnitine

    AMINO ACID DERIVATIVE FOR ENERGY AND BRAIN FUNCTION

    Acetyl-L-Carnitine is an acetylated form of L-carnitine that is believed to support mitochondrial function and energy production in the brain. It may help with mental energy and clarity.

  • 10

    DMAE

    COMPOUND FOR COGNITIVE SUPPORT

    DMAE (dimethylaminoethanol) is a naturally occurring compound that is often included in cognitive supplements for its potential to support attention and mood, though research is ongoing.
